# Skill: /interview

Prepare for an upcoming interview based on a tracked application.

## Step 1: Load Context
- Find the application in `documents/applications/`
- Load: posting snapshot, submitted CV, submitted cover letter
- Load any company research from `company_research/`

## Step 2: Build Prep Pack

### Company Overview
- What they do, their products, recent news
- Culture and values

### Role Analysis
- Key responsibilities from the posting
- Must-have skills vs. nice-to-haves

### Likely Interview Questions
Map to STAR examples from Vishnu's experience:

| Question | STAR Example | Key Points |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| Tell me about yourself | — | Focus on SDET journey |
| Describe a bug you found | — | Automation catch |
| How do you handle tight deadlines? | — | ProTouch delivery |

### Questions to Ask the Interviewer
- What does success look like in this role in 90 days?
- What is the team's current test coverage?
- What CI/CD pipeline do you use?

## Step 3: Mock Interview (optional)
If user says "mock interview", roleplay as the interviewer:
- Start with: "Tell me about yourself"
- Follow with JD-relevant technical and behavioral questions
- Give feedback after each answer
