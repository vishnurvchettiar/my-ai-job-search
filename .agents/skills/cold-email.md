# Skill: /cold-email

Generate a personalized cold email to a hiring manager or recruiter when there is no open job posting.

## When to use
- No job listing found but the company is a target
- Reaching out directly to a recruiter on LinkedIn/email
- Following up after a networking event

## Step 1: Research Target
- Company: What do they do, recent news, tech stack (check company_research/)
- Recipient: Name, title, LinkedIn URL if provided
- Context: Any mutual connection or referral?

## Step 2: Write the Cold Email

### Structure (KEEP IT SHORT — under 150 words)
```
Subject: [Specific, non-generic subject line]

Hi [First Name],

[1 sentence opener — something specific about them/their company, not generic praise]

[1-2 sentences: who you are + your most relevant skill/achievement]

[1 sentence: what you're looking for + why their company specifically]

[Clear CTA: "Would you have 15 minutes this week?"]

Best,
Vishnu Chettiar
[Phone] | [LinkedIn] | [GitHub]
```

## Rules
- NEVER start with "I hope this email finds you well"
- NEVER use "I am a passionate developer"
- Reference something SPECIFIC about the company (product, blog post, news)
- One CTA only — do not give multiple options
- No attachments in first email (offer to share CV if interested)

## Step 3: LinkedIn DM variant (even shorter, 80 words max)
Also produce a LinkedIn message version if requested.

## Step 4: Save
Save to `cover_letters/cold_email_<CompanyName>_<Date>.md`
Record company in `documents/job_tracker.csv` with status "cold_email_sent"
